# Coverage

## Inclusion test

A regime is recorded here only if it publishes a standing third-party access regime — an enrollment path, a build surface, an instrumentation surface, and a read/export surface with named roles — and someone has done the sourced work of writing it up. See `README.md` for the full inclusion policy and the reasoning behind it.

> **Absence of a record is not a finding about an environment.** Regimes are recorded when they publish a standing third-party access regime and someone has done the work. Nothing here rates, ranks, or compares environments or operators, and no inference should be drawn from an absence.

## Scope statement

This directory covers platform ecosystems that sell or publish standing third-party building as a product — the kind of arrangement a developer or brand can enroll in without negotiating a bespoke deal. It does not, and will not, cover:

- Environments where third-party access is negotiated per deal (no standing program to document).
- Undocumented or informal partner arrangements, even where widely known to exist.
- Anything not sourced to the operator's own published documentation.

A regime's presence or absence here is a statement about documentation, not about the environment, the operator, or the opportunity.
